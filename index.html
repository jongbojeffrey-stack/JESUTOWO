Dear Dr Jesutowo,

As you prepare for your First MB, I just want to wish you the very best. This is a meaningful step in your journey, and reaching this point is already something worth acknowledging. I hope you give yourself credit for the work it has taken to get here.

There may be long reading days, challenging topics and moments when everything feels like a lot. When those moments come, take a breath, regroup and keep going. You do not have to conquer the entire mountain in one day; just keep taking the next step, one topic and one effort at a time.

May you have a clear mind as you study, a sharp memory when you revise, and calm confidence when you sit for your papers. May the things you have prepared come back to you at the right moments, and may your hard work meet the opportunity it deserves.

May God go before you, strengthen you and give you wisdom throughout this season. May He bless your preparation, settle every anxious thought and grant you success in your First MB. May you come through it safely, joyfully and in flying colours.

Keep believing in the value of your effort. Progress is not always loud or obvious, but every focused hour counts. Stay steady, trust the process and remember that this examination is one chapter in a much bigger story of all you can become.

May courage carry you through the difficult pages, and may success be waiting at the end of this chapter.

With warm wishes and every good thought,

Jeffrey ♡
